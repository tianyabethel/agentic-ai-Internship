# Internship Reflection

This internship gave me the opportunity to build a real estate AI assistant and see how individual AI features can come together into one larger system. Throughout the internship, I worked with natural language property search, market statistics, recommendations, semantic search, RAG, conversational memory, and multi agent orchestration. Bringing these different pieces together helped me better understand how AI can be applied to practical business problems.

One of the biggest challenges I experienced during the internship was getting the WhatsApp interface properly integrated with the rest of the system. I had to work through issues with the interface and also make sure the assistant was providing useful information without giving unnecessary information in its responses. There were several points where the different parts of the project did not work exactly as expected, but I am happy that I pushed through the challenges and worked through the different issues until I was able to bring the project together.

I also developed a much greater appreciation for how much work goes into implementing AI workflows. A well rounded assistant is not just one AI model answering questions. There are multiple features, workflows, and agents working together to handle different types of requests. Each part has its own purpose, and getting everything to work together requires testing, troubleshooting, and attention to how the system responds to users.

I became more comfortable working with JavaScript, Python, SQL data, APIs, embeddings, and AI agent workflows. Working with real estate data also showed me how important data quality is when building analytics features. Making sure the system is using the correct information and handling situations where information is missing or unreliable is an important part of building a useful AI application.

If I continued developing the project, I would improve the conversational memory and make the interactions between agents more robust. I would also expand the evaluation dataset with more realistic conversations and additional edge cases. I would continue improving the WhatsApp experience so that the assistant provides clear and relevant responses while avoiding unnecessary information.

Overall, I am proud that I was able to push through the challenges of the project and complete the different pieces of the final capstone. The internship gave me a better understanding of the technical work involved in building AI workflows and showed me that creating a well rounded AI assistant requires many different components working together. It also gave me more confidence in my ability to troubleshoot problems, learn new tools, and continue working through technical challenges when things do not work on the first attempt.
